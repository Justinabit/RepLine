THE MECHANICS

How It Works

What is the challenge?

Do as many push-ups as you can, back to back, without stopping. Your score is the number of valid, consecutive repetitions counted by the camera before you rest or finish.

How does the camera count reps?

REP LINE runs on-device pose detection (MediaPipe Pose Landmarker) to find your shoulders, elbows, wrists and hips 30+ times a second. It measures your elbow angle over time and only counts a rep after a full up → down → up cycle — never from a single frame or a small twitch.

What counts as a valid push-up?

Starting near-straight arms (top position), lowering until your elbow angle passes the bottom threshold, then pushing back up past the top threshold again. Partial dips, wiggling, or standing up don't complete the cycle, so they aren't counted.

How should I position my camera?

Prop your phone or laptop at roughly waist height, to your side, far enough back that your whole body — shoulders to ankles — fits in frame. Landscape orientation usually works best on phones.

Why wasn't my rep counted?

Usually one of: part of your body left the frame, lighting was too low for confident tracking, or the movement didn't reach a full top or bottom position. Watch the on-screen tracking quality and form feedback — they'll tell you what to fix in real time.

How is the leaderboard calculated?

Highest completed-challenge score wins, sorted descending, with ties sharing a rank. It's a local leaderboard stored in this browser — see the leaderboard page for what that means and what it would take to make it fully shared.

ABOUT

About REP LINE

REP LINE is a camera-based push-up challenge tracker designed to make push-up competitions simple, interactive, and fun.

The website uses real-time pose detection to track body movement and automatically count valid push-up repetitions.

What You Can Do

  • Start a push-up challenge
  • Track repetitions using your camera
  • Set personal records
  • View challenge history
  • Compare scores on the leaderboard
  • Challenge yourself and compete with friends

How It Works

  1. Allow camera access
  2. Position your body so it's clearly visible
  3. Start the challenge
  4. Pose detection tracks your movement
  5. Valid push-ups are counted automatically
  6. Finish to save your result
  7. View your score and leaderboard position
